Dining and Catering Operations Support in a Cross-Cultural Team Environment

Contributed to campus dining and catered-event operations across food preparation, service support, cleanup, and fast-paced coordination with teammates from varied cultural backgrounds.

Overview

In this on-campus role, I worked with the University Housing Dining & Catering Center, which supports both daily campus dining services and catering for university events. The work involved practical day-to-day operations rather than technical development, but it was an important part of how I learned to operate reliably in a fast-paced, service-oriented team environment.

Responsibilities included assisting with food preparation, organizing ingredients and supplies, supporting meal service during events, clearing dishes, and helping with cleanup after service. The work required attention to pace, coordination, and consistency, especially when multiple tasks had to be handled under time pressure in kitchen and event settings.

More broadly, the experience gave me sustained exposure to a cross-cultural workplace outside the classroom and lab. Working with teammates from different backgrounds strengthened my communication, adaptability, and ability to contribute effectively in shared operational environments where reliability and collaboration matter as much as individual effort.
